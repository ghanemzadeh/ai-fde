# Weekly Status Template

From *Forward Deployed AI Engineering* by Nasser Ghanemzadeh — Appendix A.5; see Chapters 12 and 16.

The weekly status update is the single most-read artifact of the engagement. It should be short and the same shape every week so the sponsor can scan it in thirty seconds. The template below is what I send my customers every Friday during an engagement.

```
WEEKLY UPDATE — [Customer name] — Week of [Date]

WHAT SHIPPED
  - [Specific change, with a link to the demo, dashboard, or PR]
  - [Specific change]

WHAT'S BLOCKED
  - [Specific blocker, with the path to unblock and the owner]
  - [Specific blocker]
  (If nothing is blocked, say so explicitly.)

WHAT WE LEARNED
  - [One or two specific things from the week that change how we
    think about the rest of the engagement]

METRIC MOVEMENT
  - [Success metric: current value vs. last week vs. baseline]
  - [Eval suite: pass rate this week vs. last week]

ONE RISK FOR NEXT WEEK
  - [The single most important thing that could go wrong, and
    what we will do to prevent it]
```
