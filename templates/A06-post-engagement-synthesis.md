# Post-Engagement Synthesis Template

From *Forward Deployed AI Engineering* by Nasser Ghanemzadeh — Appendix A.6; see Chapters 7 and 12.

The synthesis document is what turns the engagement into institutional memory. Writing it is what produces the highway from the gravel road. The template below is the minimum format I use; expand any section that has more material to record.

```
ENGAGEMENT SYNTHESIS — [Customer name] — [End date]

ENGAGEMENT OVERVIEW
  - One-paragraph summary of the problem, the solution, and the outcome.
  - Total duration: [N weeks]
  - Contract value: [$]
  - Success metric movement: [baseline → final]

GRAVEL-ROAD INVENTORY
  Customer-specific work shipped during this engagement:
  - [Component name]: [what it does, why this customer needed it]
  - [Component name]: [what it does, why this customer needed it]

HIGHWAY CANDIDATES
  Patterns from this engagement that should be considered for paving
  into the core product:
  - [Pattern]: [why generalizable, what the next two customers who
    will need it look like]
  - [Pattern]: [same]

EVAL-SET LEARNINGS
  - New eval cases added: [N]
  - Old eval cases retired: [N]
  - Calibration changes to LLM-judge: [summary]
  - Rubric changes: [summary, with rationale]

WHAT THE CUSTOMER SURPRISED US BY CARING ABOUT
  - [Specific item — usually the thing nobody mentioned in scoping
    but that turned out to determine adoption]

WHAT WAS HARD
  - [Honest description of the engagement's hardest moments and
    how they were resolved]

WHAT WE WOULD DO DIFFERENTLY
  - [Specific decisions, with the better alternative named]

HANDOFF STATUS
  - Customer team members trained: [names]
  - Runbook delivered and walked through: [Y/N]
  - Eval suite ownership transferred: [Y/N]
  - Escalation path for incidents documented: [Y/N]

OPEN QUESTIONS
  - [Items the engagement raised that the current product does not
    yet have a clean answer for]
```
