# Standard playbook (invented)

An invented playbook for the sample eval set. It stands in for the customer's own standard positions. Replace it with the real one.

A clause diverges when it departs from a position below. Positions marked **material** are the ones the customer cannot accept a miss on.

| Topic | Standard position | Risk if it diverges | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liability cap | Each party's liability is capped at no less than twelve months of fees. | High | Yes |
| Indemnity | Each party indemnifies the other for its own negligence only. | High | Yes |
| Structural repairs | The landlord carries structural repairs. | High | Yes |
| Rule changes | A change to building rules cannot create a default without notice and a chance to cure. | High | Yes |
| Customer data | The vendor may not keep or reuse the customer's data or footage. | High | Yes |
| Renewal | Automatic renewal needs no more than 60 days notice to cancel. | Medium | No |
| Fee increases | Annual increases are capped at 3 percent. | Medium | No |
| Operating costs | The tenant's share of operating costs has an annual cap. | Medium | No |
| Assignment | Assignment needs the other party's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. | Medium | No |
| Payment terms | Invoices are due within 30 days. | Low | No |
