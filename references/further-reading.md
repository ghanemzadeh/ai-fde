# Further Reading by Theme

Reading paths from Appendix C of *Forward Deployed AI Engineering*. Sources marked "see Ref" are also in the [numbered reference list](references.md). The rest informed the argument without being cited directly and are recommended as background.

## Foundational sources

- *Reflections on Palantir.* Nabeel S. Qureshi (October 2024). (see [Ref 24](references.md#ref-24))
- *The Path to Palantir (Entire Talk).* Stephen Cohen, Stanford eCorner (February 2013). (see [Ref 30](references.md#ref-30))
- *The Rise of Human-Computer Cooperation.* Shyam Sankar, TEDGlobal 2012. <https://ted.com/talks/shyam_sankar_the_rise_of_human_computer_cooperation>. The earliest public articulation of the human-in-the-loop justification for the role.
- *An Interview with Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar and Head of Global Commercial Ted Mabrey.* Ben Thompson, Stratechery (June 15, 2023). The interview that gave the wider world the FDE-as-secret-weapon framing.
- *Sorry, That Isn't an FDE.* Ted Mabrey (September 20, 2024). (see [Ref 84](references.md#ref-84))
- *Shyam Sankar — Celebrating Heretics.* Patrick O'Shaughnessy, *Invest Like the Best* EP.462 (March 2026). (see [Ref 50](references.md#ref-50))
- *Why We Built Palantir.* Joe Lonsdale (September 2020). <https://blog.joelonsdale.com/p/why-we-built-palantir>.
- *The Technological Republic.* Alexander C. Karp and Nicholas W. Zamiska. Crown Currency, February 2025. The ideological scaffolding behind Palantir's hire-for-mission FDE culture.
- *Impro: Improvisation and the Theatre.* Keith Johnstone, Faber and Faber, 1979. (see [Ref 48](references.md#ref-48)) On Palantir's onboarding reading list; the precise vocabulary for the FDE temperament — accepting offers, reading status, dropping the armor.
